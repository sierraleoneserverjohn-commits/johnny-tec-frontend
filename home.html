Explain quantum computing in simple terms.

Quantum computing is a type of computing that uses the principles of quantum mechanics to process information. Unlike classical computers that use bits (0s and 1s), quantum computers use quantum bits or qubits.


Qubits can exist in multiple states simultaneously thanks to a property called superposition. This allows quantum computers to solve certain problems much faster than traditional computers.
